Return & Refund Policy

At GetOrgi, customer satisfaction is at the heart of everything we do. We ensure the highest standards in sourcing and delivering fresh, homemade, and organic products across Kerala. Here's everything you need to know about our return and refund policies.

1. Return Eligibility

  • Packaged or Non-Perishable Products: Returns are accepted if the product you received is damaged, defective, expired, or incorrect.
  • Perishable Products (Fruits, Vegetables, Fresh Goods): Due to their delicate and time-sensitive nature, these items are non-returnable. However, if you face any issues, please contact us immediately — your satisfaction matters!

2. How to Initiate a Return

To request a return or report an issue, please contact us within 7 days of receiving your order via WhatsApp or Email:

  • WhatsApp: +91 6282 954 359 (Quick Support)
  • Email: getorgifresh@gmail.com

Please include your order number, product details, and clear images (if applicable) to help us process your request faster.

3. Refunds

Once we verify the issue, refunds will be processed quickly. The amount will be refunded to your original method of payment (bank account, UPI, or card) within 5-7 business days. For fruits, vegetables, or fresh produce concerns, after verification via WhatsApp communication, we directly process a refund — no need to return the item.

4. Exchanges

In special cases, if the item is eligible and available, we can offer an exchange instead of a refund. Exchanges are subject to stock availability and product type.

5. Cancellations

Orders can be cancelled if the request is made before dispatch. Once the product is out for delivery, cancellations are not possible, especially for perishable goods.

6. Important Notes

  • We pack all products with utmost care. If a product gets damaged during shipping, kindly contact us immediately.
  • Products must be unused, sealed (if applicable), and in original condition to qualify for a return (except for fresh items).
  • Refunds/returns are solely applicable for purchases made directly through GetOrgi.com.
