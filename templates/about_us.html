Welcome to the GetOrgi Family

At GetOrgi, we are not just an online store — we are a community, a family, and a movement toward healthier living. Our mission is simple: to connect you with authentic, organic, and lovingly homemade products, sourced locally from the passionate hearts of Kerala.

Our Story 🌱

Founded by Achu Joseph S L in Thiruvananthapuram (TVM), Kerala, GetOrgi was born from a simple but powerful idea: to celebrate and support the hardworking local producers who believe in purity, passion, and sustainability. We recognized the need for genuine, homemade goodness in a world full of artificial choices — and decided to create a trusted space for it.

Every item we offer is crafted or sourced with care, ensuring that when you shop with us, you are bringing home something real, fresh, and nourishing.

What We Offer 🌾

  • Directly Sourced from Passionate Local Producers
  • Fast Delivery Across Kerala (Within 3-4 Days)
  • Family-Focused Values and a Healthy Lifestyle Mission
  • Growing Startup Powered by Your Trust and Blessings

Our Commitment to You 💚
